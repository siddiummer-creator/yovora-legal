Privacy Policy for Yovora

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yovora ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

1. Who We Are

Yovora is operated by Shithin T Ummer ("the Developer"), based in Germany. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can contact us at:

Email: hello.yovora@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use Yovora:

Account information. Your email address, password (stored in encrypted/hashed form), and any display name or profile photo you choose to provide.

Health and fitness data. Information you log within the App, including workouts, exercises, sets and reps, nutrition and food entries, calorie and macro goals, water intake, sleep records, step counts, body measurements, progress photos, supplement intake, and habit-tracking entries.

Social and content data. Posts, comments, likes, messages, follower/following relationships, and any other content you create or share within the App's social features.

Technical and usage data. Device type, operating system version, app version, and crash or error logs, which help us maintain and improve the App.

We do not collect more information than is necessary to provide the App's features, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and operate the App's core features (tracking, social feed, messaging, and related functionality)
  • Calculate personalized metrics such as calorie and macro targets based on the information you provide
  • Maintain your account and authenticate your access
  • Display your content to other users where you have chosen to share it (for example, social feed posts)
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve the App's performance and reliability
  • Communicate with you about your account when necessary

We do not use your health or fitness data for advertising purposes, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we process your personal data on the following legal bases: your consent (for example, when you choose to share content publicly within the App), the performance of a contract (providing the App's services to you as a registered user), and our legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the App.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your data is stored on secure, access-controlled servers using industry-standard practices, including encrypted password storage and secured database access. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with third-party service providers who help us operate the App (for example, cloud hosting and media storage providers), solely for the purpose of providing the App's functionality, and only to the extent necessary. These providers are obligated to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Yovora, our users, or others.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated data (available directly within the App via Settings, or by contacting us)
  • Export your data in a portable format, upon request
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, you can use the relevant feature within the App or contact us at hello.yovora@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may delete your account at any time directly within the App (Settings → Delete Account). Deleting your account will permanently remove your profile, logged health and fitness data, posts, and messages from our active systems. Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, after which it will be permanently erased.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide you the App's services. If you delete your account, your data will be deleted in accordance with Section 8 above.

10. Children's Privacy

Yovora is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. International Data Transfers

If your data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we take steps to ensure it remains protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law, including the use of appropriate safeguards where required.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by other reasonable means. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:

Email: hello.yovora@gmail.com
